Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy applies to the Stackline games published by Pilotx (including Prism, Rift, and related titles under the app.stackline.* package family). Contact: pilotxpil@gmail.com.

What the games do

These are offline-first casual games. Progress, coins, themes, and settings are stored locally on your device. There is no account and no sign-in.

Advertising

Free versions may show ads through Google AdMob (rewarded video and interstitial). AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers (including advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP, and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads, subject to your device and regional consent choices.

You can remove ads with an optional in-app purchase where offered.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (remove ads, themes, coin packs) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Data we do not collect ourselves

We do not operate our own analytics backend for these games. We do not sell personal information. Game saves stay on the device unless you back up your device with a third-party service you choose.

Children

Most Stackline games are intended for a general audience age 13 and up and are not directed at children under 13.

Cubemate (app.stackline.cubemate) is a chess game made for families and is also intended for children age 6 and up. In Cubemate:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game has no account, chat, social features or location access.
  • Ads are shown through Google AdMob configured for child-directed treatment and users under the age of consent: ads are not personalized, the maximum ad content rating is General (G), and interest-based advertising and remarketing are not used.
  • Only AdMob-certified family-friendly ad content is requested; AdMob may process limited device data (such as IP address and diagnostics) to deliver and measure ads, as described in Google's policies.
  • Purchases (coins, remove ads, gold theme) are processed by Google Play. Parents can use Google Play parental controls to restrict purchases.
  • Game progress is stored only on the device and is deleted when the app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Parents or guardians who have questions, or who believe a child has provided personal information, can contact pilotxpil@gmail.com and we will act on the request.

Your choices

  • Reset or clear app data to delete local saves.
  • Use OS advertising / privacy controls to limit ad personalization.
  • Uninstall the app at any time.

Changes

We may update this policy when features or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
